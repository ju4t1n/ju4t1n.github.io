About

I am a master's student in Civil Engineering at Chongqing University, advised by Prof. Jiepeng Liu and Prof. Pengkun Liu. My research focuses on intelligent perception and digital modeling in civil engineering scenarios, exploring the use of computer vision and large-scale visual models for point cloud segmentation, and combining multi-agent collaborative systems to automatically generate building information models (BIM), in order to enhance the automation construction capabilities and engineering practicality of digital twin models.

Based on the current research, I hope to further explore the application of robots in the civil engineering environment, particularly focusing on infrastructure inspection and disease screening. At the same time, I am also interested in knowledge graphs, and I hope to study how to organize inspection objects, information, and their relationships into structured knowledge to support intelligent inspection and digital twin applications.
